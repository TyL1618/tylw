簡介 / Introduction

資訊工程 CS 背景出身。現在於單晶片控制公司，負責材料特性測試儀器的整套軟體：GUI、資料庫、App 與 PWA、Python 技術相關及網頁。

儀器的晶片與通訊協議由公司內部設計，我負責串接各式自訂通訊協議。

此網站最早由我高中時手刻建立，而後來陸續放進大學的課程作業及專題。這裡也是自己的一張名片，放一些介紹自己的作品。

Computer science background. I currently work at a company specializing in single-chip control, where I am responsible for the complete software of materials-testing instruments: GUI, database, App and PWA, Python tooling, and web.

The instruments' chips and communication protocols are designed in-house; I integrate the various custom communication protocols.

This site was first built by hand in my high school years, and I later added coursework and projects from university. It is also my personal business card, a place to showcase my work.
